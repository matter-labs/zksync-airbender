## V3 findings awaiting v2 assessment

Bigint control masks, unissued workers, worker ROM ports, decoder filler outside the checked ADD/SUB, word-memory and control minus-one profiles,
terminal-PC closure beyond the checked arithmetic/word-memory paths and supported-mask local control bounds, and contribution-mask/padding conditions must be checked
against native v2 code and artifacts before adopting a finding or a temporary
assumption. The local control assessment above does not establish initialized control/whole-machine terminal soundness or adopt the source closure interface.
