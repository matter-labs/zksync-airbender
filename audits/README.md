# Veridise tooling assessment of Airbender v2

The [Veridise report](VAR_zksync_260216_picus_fuzzing-Veridise.pdf) records an
assessment conducted from February 16 to April 15, 2026, against Airbender v2 at
commit [5ecb67472a3110878417527398d7e3a43875e57c](https://github.com/matter-labs/zksync-airbender/commit/5ecb67472a3110878417527398d7e3a43875e57c).

The scope covered selected Picus/LLZK circuit determinism checks, fuzzing of
execution and proving components, and targeted manual follow-up. It was not a
comprehensive end-to-end manual audit.

The results apply to the assessed revision and the fixes identified in the report.
They do not establish coverage of later v2 changes or of v3 (`av_gkr_compiler`).
